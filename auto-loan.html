Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What sort of loans can I use this EMI calculator for?
You can use this calculator for Auto Loans, Car Loans, Two-Wheeler Loans, Personal Loans, and Home Financing[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
How does the debt-to-income ratio affect loan eligibility?
Banks prefer your total monthly debt payments (including the new car EMI) to remain below 50% of your net monthly income to maximize approval chances[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
What does an EMI consist of?
Every Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) comprises a portion of the principal repayment alongside the calculated interest for that specific month[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
Is reducing balance interest better than flat interest?
Yes, reducing balance interest charges interest only on the outstanding principal balance left after previous repayments, saving you considerable money compared to flat interest methods.
