Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I calculate home loans in multiple global currencies?
Yes, use our multi-currency selector to dynamically switch between USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, SGD, AED, JPY, CHF, CNY, NZD, and HKD.
How does property down payment affect my housing loan EMI?
A larger down payment reduces the total mortgage principal required, resulting in a lower monthly EMI and reduced overall interest burden.
What additional costs should I factor into a home loan?
Additional expenses include stamp duty, registration fees, legal documentation charges, and home insurance.
How can I export my home loan repayment schedule?
You can instantly download your month-wise amortization schedule as a CSV file or print/save it as a PDF report.
What is the difference between reducing and flat interest rates?
Reducing interest applies only to the remaining balance, whereas flat interest charges interest on the original loan amount throughout the entire term.
How do URL parameters work for saving calculations?
You can pass parameters directly in the URL such as ?loan=3500000&rate=8.5&tenure=20&type=reducing to preload specific calculator states.
Is this home loan calculator free to use?
Yes, our calculator is 100% free, fast, responsive, and accessible globally without restrictions.
Can I share my calculated results with others?
Yes, use the native device share capability or copy the current link directly to your clipboard.
