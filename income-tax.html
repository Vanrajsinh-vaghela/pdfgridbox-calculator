Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How is income up to ₹12 Lakh effectively tax-free under the New Tax Regime?
With a Gross Salary of ₹12.75 Lakh, subtracting the ₹75,000 Standard Deduction brings Taxable Income to ₹12 Lakh. Under Section 87A, a full rebate of up to ₹60,000 applies, making tax ₹0.
What are the New Tax Regime Slabs for FY 2026-27?
Up to ₹4 Lakh: Nil | ₹4L - ₹8L: 5% | ₹8L - ₹12L: 10% | ₹12L - ₹16L: 15% | ₹16L - ₹20L: 20% | ₹20L - ₹24L: 25% | Above ₹24L: 30%.
What is the Standard Deduction available for salaried employees?
A Standard Deduction of ₹75,000 is available under the New Tax Regime, while ₹50,000 is available under the Old Tax Regime.
Can I switch from Old to New Tax Regime?
Yes, salaried employees can choose every year. Business taxpayers require Form 10-IEA to switch regimes.
