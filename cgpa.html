Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How is CGPA converted into percentage?
For CBSE and most Indian universities, multiply your CGPA by 9.5 to get the equivalent percentage (Percentage = CGPA × 9.5).
How does credit weightage affect CGPA?
Courses with higher credit hours have a larger impact on your overall CGPA. Weighted CGPA = Total (Grade Point × Credit) / Total Credits.
